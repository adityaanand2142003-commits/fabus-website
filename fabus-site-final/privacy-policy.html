Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains, in plain terms, what information FABUS Global collects through this website and how it's used. It is not a substitute for legal advice.

Who we are

FABUS Global ("FABUS," "we," "us") is a homeware sourcing company connecting retailers, brands, wholesalers, and importers with manufacturing partners across India and Asia. This policy covers the website at fabusglobal.com and its subpages.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Contact details submitted through our Supply Chain Scorecard (name, business email, company, country)
  • Information submitted through our Supply With Us supplier application form (company details, contact information, uploaded documents)
  • Any information you send us directly via email or WhatsApp

We also automatically collect limited technical information when you browse our site, such as pages visited and general usage patterns, to help us understand how the site is used.

How we use your information

  • To respond to inquiries and provide requested information or quotes
  • To evaluate supplier applications submitted through our forms
  • To deliver your Supply Chain Scorecard results
  • To improve our website and services
  • To communicate with you about our services, where appropriate

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate this website and process form submissions (for example, Google's form-processing and spreadsheet infrastructure)
  • Legal or regulatory authorities, where required by law

Cookies and analytics

This site uses cookies to support basic functionality and to help us understand site traffic. We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregate information about how visitors use our site — such as pages viewed, time spent, and general location (country/city level) — which helps us improve the site. This data is not used to personally identify individual visitors. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings at any time, and you can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using Google's browser opt-out tool.

International data transfers

FABUS Global operates across India, Asia, and the United States, and the third-party services we use (such as Google's infrastructure) may store or process information in countries other than your own. By using this website, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in a country with different data protection laws than your own.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information submitted to us, but no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and use of this website is at your own risk in that respect.

Data retention

We retain information submitted through our forms for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, evaluate a supplier relationship, or comply with our legal and business obligations.

Your rights

Depending on where you're located, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, or to object to how it's used. You can exercise any of these rights, or withdraw your consent to our processing of your information at any time, by contacting us using the details below. If you withdraw consent, it won't affect anything we did with your information before that point, and we may be unable to respond to a form submission you've already made if the underlying information is removed.

Children's privacy

This website and our services are intended for business use and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.
